Attention All Policyholders

Payment of premium by Cheque/Demand Draft

The cheque /DD should be drawn favouring “LIC of India, Pol no. xxxxxxxxx on the face of
the instrument in case of Single Cheque. Whenever single cheque is used for payment of

multiple policies, one policy number is to be written on the face of the instrument and other
numbers may be written on the reverse of the cheque.
